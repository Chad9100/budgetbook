Auto-categorisation rules

A rule says payee contains X → category Y (case-insensitive). It can also match when the payee starts with X, is exactly X, or matches a regular expression. An optional amount range (size only, ignoring whether it is spending or income) limits a rule to larger or smaller transactions. When several rules match, an exact rule wins, then the longest "contains" or "starts with" pattern, then a regex rule. Rules apply to new transactions without a category, to imports, and on demand below.
